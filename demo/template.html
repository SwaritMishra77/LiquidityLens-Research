Evaluation design

Separate dates. Separate companies.

The primary target is next-quarter operating cash flow divided by revenue. The secondary target is a cash-flow stress proxy over two future quarters.

Historical boundaries

Training: 2015–2020. Validation: 2021–2022. Calibration: 2023–2024. Evaluation: 2025. Labels not yet published at a development boundary are purged.

Company holdout

Availability

Financial facts are filtered by filing date. Publication time uses a conservative filing-date-plus-two-days guard. Forecasts require at least 14 days remaining before the target quarter ends.

Interpretation

What this pilot can tell us

Complex models are compared with seasonal, linear and gradient-boosted baselines. FinBERT and disclosure-change features are evaluated on a smaller, identical set of rows.

Uncertainty

80% is nominal interval coverage. Actual historical coverage can differ. Serial dependence, changing conditions and few stress events limit reliability claims.

Scope limits

A purposive 50-company sample, incomplete standard-tag coverage, a smaller disclosure subset and changes in corporate perimeter limit generalisation. Negative CFO may reflect seasonality or planned investment in operations.

Development history

Data reconciliation and evaluation windows were refined during development. Results are exploratory historical evidence; they are not untouched confirmatory validation or realised business impact.

MAE is in percentage points. Neural results average three seeds. “—” indicates an undefined ranking metric when only one outcome class is present.
